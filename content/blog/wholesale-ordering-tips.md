# 5 Tips for Your First Wholesale Palm Order

Placing your first bulk order for artificial palm trees? Follow these tips for a smooth experience.

## 1. Request Samples First

Always order 1–2 samples before committing to a large quantity. We ship samples within 7 business days.

## 2. Understand MOQ

Our standard MOQ is 20 units per SKU. Custom OEM orders start at 50 units. Mixed-SKU orders may qualify for reduced MOQ — ask your sales rep.

## 3. Plan for Shipping Lead Time

- Sea freight: 25–35 days
- Air freight: 7–10 days
- Express sample: 3–5 days

## 4. Prepare for Customs

We provide commercial invoices, packing lists, and HS codes. Check your country's import duties for artificial plants (typically HS Code 6702).

## 5. Communicate Your Project Timeline

Share your installation date with us early. We can prioritize production and recommend the best shipping method to meet your deadline.
