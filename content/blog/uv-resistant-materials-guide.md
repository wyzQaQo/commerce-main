# UV-Resistant Materials: A Buyer's Guide

When sourcing artificial palm trees for outdoor use, understanding UV resistance is critical for product longevity.

## What Is UV Rating?

UV rating indicates how long a product maintains its color and structural integrity under direct sunlight. Our standard outdoor products carry a **5+ year UV guarantee**.

## Material Types

### PE (Polyethylene)
The industry standard for outdoor foliage. Flexible, realistic, and excellent UV stability.

### PVC (Polyvinyl Chloride)
More rigid, often used for trunks and structural elements. Requires UV stabilizer additives for outdoor use.

### Steel Core
Internal steel framework provides wind resistance. Always powder-coated to prevent rust.

## Questions to Ask Your Supplier

1. What UV stabilizer additives are used?
2. Is there a written UV warranty?
3. Has the product been tested in coastal/salt environments?
4. Can you provide installation guidelines for maximum longevity?

Our quality team is happy to provide material certificates and test reports upon request.
